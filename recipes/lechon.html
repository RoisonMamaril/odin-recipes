Lechon Kawali

Description

There are many ways to cook Lechon Kawali. This is the version I make during Christmas and special occasions because the pork turns juicy and tender while the skin crisps up brown and crackly.

On Christmas Eve, the kitchen is busy and this dish is always a hit. Boiling the pork belly first with bay leaves, peppercorns, and pork cube flavors the meat, and a quick deep fry at the end gives the texture contrast that makes each bite delicious.

Ingredients

  • Pork belly
  • Knorr pork cube
  • Dried bay leaves
  • Whole peppercorns
  • Knorr Liquid Seasoning
  • Water
  • Cooking oil

Steps

  1. Boil water in a pot, then add bay leaves, peppercorns, and the pork cube.
  2. Add the pork belly, cover, and simmer on medium heat for 45 minutes until tender.
  3. Remove the pork belly and let it cool on a clean plate.
  4. Rub Knorr Liquid Seasoning all over the cooled pork and let it rest for 30 minutes.
  5. Heat oil in a deep pot to about 300°F, then carefully lower the pork belly into the hot oil.
  6. Fry the pork for 7 to 10 minutes, then turn it and fry the other side for about 8 minutes until the skin is crisp and brown.
  7. Remove the crispy pork, drain on a wire rack or paper towel, let it cool slightly, then slice.
  8. Arrange on a serving plate and enjoy with your favorite dipping sauce or enjoy it plain.
