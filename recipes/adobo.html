Pork Adobo

Description

Pork Adobo is a dish wherein pork is simmered in soy sauce and vinegar. This is considered a favorite in Filipino households. Using pork belly helps the fat melt into the sauce while the meat becomes tender and rich, making it perfect over hot rice.

At home, pork adobo often stretched beyond dinner. Leftovers were packed for school, reheated for breakfast, and served with garlic fried rice and egg. The sauce should be dark, slightly sharp from vinegar, and rich from the pork fat.

Ingredients

  • Pork belly, cut into bite-size pieces
  • Garlic, crushed
  • Dried bay leaves
  • White vinegar
  • Soy sauce
  • Whole peppercorns
  • Water
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Marinate the pork with soy sauce and crushed garlic for at least 1 hour.
  2. Heat a pot over medium heat and brown the pork pieces, then add the reserved marinade.
  3. Add water, peppercorns, and bay leaves, then bring to a boil.
  4. Lower the heat and simmer for 40 minutes to 1 hour, until the pork is tender.
  5. Pour in the vinegar without stirring, let it boil, then stir gently.
  6. Simmer uncovered for another 12 to 15 minutes, taste, add salt if needed, and serve hot over rice.
